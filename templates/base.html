Track Pulse is a Smart India Hackathon 2026 prototype for problem SIH26028, by Team Tech Sprints.

Run history from the Kaggle dataset “Indian Railways: stop-level delay history” (GPL-3.0), live positions from the whereismytrain.in public cache with RailRadar as backup. Neither is official Indian Railways data.
